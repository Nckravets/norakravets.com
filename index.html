About me

Code, Creativity, & Community

In the old days, you were told to choose. Left brain or right. Code or canvas. The lab or the studio.

I never could.

So I'm finishing a B.S. in Computer Science at the University of San Francisco with a minor in Fine Arts, and graduating in May 2027.

Right now I'm a software engineering intern at Relos, building an AI chat agent that lives inside a real estate platform. I own the chat infrastructure and the conversation between server and client, and I design the database changes underneath it in Prisma and PostgreSQL.

When I'm not at a keyboard, I'm usually at a pottery wheel. I throw and handbuild ceramics, glaze my own pieces, and once led a high school ceramics club whose motto was Carpe Lutum: seize the clay.

I've taught sewing in the summers and math during the school year. Both taught me the same thing: any big problem gets friendlier once you break it into small, clear steps.

I also like building community. I co-lead community building for Women in Technology at USF and help run compsigh, a weekly gathering for USF's CS students.

It turns out art and technology get along just fine. They only needed an introduction.
